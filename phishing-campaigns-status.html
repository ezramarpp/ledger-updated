Protect yourself

Four checks that stop most scams.

Phishing relies on urgency, imitation and confusion. Slow down whenever a message asks you to secure, verify, unlock or restore an account.

01 · TRANSACTIONS

Approve only actions you started

Never validate a transaction on your Ledger device if you did not initiate it. Reject unexpected requests and disconnect from the application.

02 · FALSE URGENCY

Your device cannot be remotely deactivated

Claims that Ledger will block or deactivate a device for KYC, security or compliance reasons are designed to frighten you into acting.

03 · OFFICIAL CHANNELS

Verify the destination independently

Do not follow links in unsolicited messages. Open a known Ledger page yourself and check the full domain before downloading software or connecting a wallet.

04 · UNEXPECTED CONTACT

Treat calls and direct messages as scams

Ledger will not call, text, WhatsApp, Telegram or privately message you to discuss account security or request secret information.

Active scam patterns

Know what attackers are using.

Formats change, but the objective is usually the same: persuade you to reveal a recovery phrase, install fake software, sign a malicious transaction or contact a fraudulent agent.

ActivePOSTAL MAIL

Letters containing QR codes

Physical letters imitate official security notices and direct recipients to scan a QR code or visit a website. The destination ultimately requests the 24-word recovery phrase. There is no legitimate reason to type those words into a computer or phone.

ActivePHONE

Fake security or recovery calls

Callers claim that a suspicious recovery, login or transfer occurred and offer to protect the account. Self-custody systems do not provide callers with visibility into phrase restoration. End the call and do not share information.

ActiveSOFTWARE

Counterfeit Ledger Wallet apps

Fake applications can closely resemble genuine wallet software. They often display a fabricated device, memory or firmware error followed by a recovery-phrase form. Close and remove the application immediately.

ActiveEMAIL & ADS

“Verify now” and protection offers

Messages use buttons such as “Secure my account,” “Verify now,” or “Claim protection device” to lead to imitation sites. Check the sender and destination, but never rely on appearance alone.

ActiveTOKENS & NFTs

Unsolicited airdrops

Unknown tokens may contain names or descriptions that promote a website. Ignore and hide them. Attempting to claim, swap or interact can lead to phishing pages or malicious contract approvals.

ActiveSOCIAL MEDIA

Impersonators in replies and DMs

Fake support accounts watch public conversations and offer private assistance. Do not move a support conversation into direct messages or follow links supplied by an unsolicited account.

If you receive a suspicious message

Pause. Preserve. Report.

Do not engage further. Keep evidence without opening attachments or visiting the supplied destination.

1

Do not click or sign

Close the page, reject device requests and stop communicating with the sender.

2

Capture the evidence

Record the sender, full URL, message, date and any transaction identifier without exposing your recovery phrase.

3

Report and block

Mark the sender as spam, notify the platform and submit the site to an appropriate phishing-report service.

Help protect the community

Report a phishing attempt.

If a message, call, website or application is impersonating Ledger, report it with as much non-sensitive detail as possible. Never include your recovery phrase, PIN or private keys.
