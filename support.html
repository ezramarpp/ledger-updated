Critical safety check

Your recovery phrase is for recovery only.

Never type the 24 words into a website, chat, form or computer application. Enter them only on a genuine Ledger device when you deliberately restore access.

01

Stop if someone asks for the words

No genuine Ledger employee or support agent needs your phrase, PIN or private keys.

02

Verify software sources

Use the official application path and inspect the publisher before installing an update.

03

Trust the device screen

Confirm the destination, amount and network on the hardware wallet—not only in the browser.

Common questions

Quick answers.

Open a question for the essential next steps.

My device is not recognized. What should I check?

Use a data-capable cable, try another USB port, unlock the device, close other wallet software and restart Ledger Wallet. On mobile, verify Bluetooth and operating-system permissions.

Can Ledger recover my PIN or recovery phrase?

No. Ledger does not know or store them. If you still have the recovery phrase, you can restore accounts on a compatible device. Without it, lost access cannot be reconstructed.

Why is my transaction still pending?

Network congestion and a low fee can delay confirmation. Verify the transaction identifier in the correct network explorer and avoid creating conflicting transfers until you understand its state.

How do I know an update is genuine?

Initiate firmware and application updates from Ledger Wallet, inspect the information shown on your device and ignore unsolicited messages linking to urgent updates.

Still stuck? Gather your device model, app version and transaction ID—never your recovery phrase.
