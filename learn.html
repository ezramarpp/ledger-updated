Security essentials

Five rules worth remembering.

Crypto transactions cannot usually be reversed. A few disciplined practices can prevent the most common losses.

01

Never share your recovery phrase

Anyone with those words can control your accounts. Legitimate support will never ask for them.

02

Verify on the trusted screen

Compare the address, amount and network shown on your hardware wallet before approving.

03

Send a test transaction

For a new address or network, send a small amount first and confirm receipt.

04

Question urgency

Scammers manufacture pressure. Pause, verify the source independently and avoid unknown links.

05

Understand permissions

Smart-contract approvals may remain active. Grant only what is needed and review them regularly.

Security becomes a habit. Start today.
